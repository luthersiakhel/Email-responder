# US

## Laptop Returns

### Return request Local to Raleigh/Durham/Cary NC
***Keywords:*** laptop, return, user local to NC
***Answer:*** 
Hello,

Laptops should be returned to Red Hat Tower in Raleigh, NC.

There are two options for laptop returns:

Drop Off: You can drop your laptop off at Red Hat Tower Monday-Friday between 10am-4pm (Excluding holidays and recharge days). You can drop it either at the Lobby Security Desk or the 14th Floor IT Help Desk.

Ship: You can request a QR Code using the form below to return your laptop. Once you receive the QR Code, all you will have to do is bring the laptop and QR Code to your nearest FedEx Office and they will package the laptop for you and ship it back to Red Hat Tower. You will not need to provide your own packaging or print a shipping label out yourself. QR Codes are processed on Mondays and will be sent to you by the end of day on Tuesdays.

Please complete [this form] (https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/290ee583f2554f00b3033148b876c260) with your choice and let us know if you have further questions.

Thanks,
Endpoint Systems

### When will I get my QR Code
***Keywords:*** QR code, haven't received
***Answer:*** 
Hello,

The FedEx shipping QR codes are generated weekly on Monday afternoons by the Logistics Team. You should receive your QR code either Monday afternoon or early Tuesday next week from noReply@FedEx.com titled “Your FedEx Shipping Information for RMA Red Hat Returns.” It will contain a list of the nearest FedEx Office locations within that email. Bring your QR Code and laptop/accessories to the nearest FedEx Office location. FedEx Office will pack and ship the laptop to Raleigh for you.

Thanks
